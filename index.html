Why it exists

Knowledge shouldn't live in a labyrinth.

Notes in one app. Sources across thirty tabs. Decisions buried in AI chats that forget you the moment they end.
The raw material of good thinking gets scattered and every day starts with finding it again.

Daedalus is the answer: one system that holds what you know, remembers what you decide and, crucially, adapts its own shape to how you work.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Supervised autonomy by choice.

Daedalus proposes; a human approves. It plans and executes real work, but nothing consequential happens without sign-off. That's not caution bolted on afterwards, it's the design principle that makes delegation trustworthy.

The knowledge base

A personal Wikipedia with provenance.

Built on an Obsidian vault with a disciplined pipeline: raw input is distilled into indexed articles and durable references, each with tracked sources and deliberate cross-links. Nothing stays a loose note; everything is findable and cited.

5
Knowledge management

Discipline is what makes it knowledge.

Research, articles, and conversations become structured, sourced, navigable knowledge, not scattered notes. Three editorial rules, applied to everything:

01 · CLASSIFY

Every capture gets a type and a home. No orphan notes.

02 · INDEX

Distilled into articles that are findable by design, not by memory.

03 · LINK

Cross-referenced so each new piece strengthens the rest.

9
Agents

Agents built for you, not shipped at you.

There is no fixed toolset. The interview picks agents from a library of 217 and installs them into your vault, where you can read every one of them as plain text and change what they do. Composing new ones visually comes later; today they are files you own.

Every agent is a file you can read, edit and delete. The gate between planning and doing is always there and always human.

12
Engineering principles

Order, by design.

01 · HUMAN GATE

Human approval before any consequential action.

02 · AUDITABLE

Every change is diffed, versioned, and reversible.

03 · DETERMINISM FIRST

Code where code is enough; AI reserved for genuine judgment.

04 · COST-CONSCIOUS

Efficient by design, models used deliberately, not by default.
